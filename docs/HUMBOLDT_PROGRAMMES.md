## Editorial decisions

- Course prices and dated departure lists are not copied into the catalogue. Duration choices are described in prose and highlights; the fixed duration field is left empty.
- Constance adult levels use A1–C1 from the location heading and school summary. The same page later mentions C2 in its general course text; no C2 group course is promised here. Executive courses have their own A1–C2 listing.
- Reimlingen horse riding is explicitly optional and charged separately. Lindenberg junior accommodation is residential; its summer host-family option applies only to ages 13–17.
- Residential and tuition-only executive options remain distinct. Exam-centre status does not imply an examination fee or preparation course is included.
- Programme descriptions, ages, highlights, accommodation information and images use the existing admin-editable fields. Open-ended adult ages are rendered as 18+ and 27+.
